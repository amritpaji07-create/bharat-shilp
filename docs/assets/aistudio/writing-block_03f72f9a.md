# PROJECT: GOVERNMENT E-COMMERCE MARKETPLACE
## Complete UI/UX Design – High-Fidelity Figma Project

### 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Design a premium, modern, trustworthy government-backed e-commerce marketplace inspired by the visual quality and shopping experience of:

- https://himira.co.in/
- https://desigud.in/

Use these websites as visual and experience references. Do not copy their exact UI, branding, content or assets.

Create an original government marketplace identity with a polished Indian e-commerce experience.

The platform should allow customers to discover and purchase products from government-approved vendors, Self Help Groups (SHGs), local manufacturers, artisans, cooperatives and registered organizations.

The website should communicate:
- Government credibility and trust.
- Indian products and local manufacturers.
- Quality and authenticity.
- Easy product discovery.
- Simple shopping and checkout.
- Support for local businesses and producers.

IMPORTANT: Prioritize beautiful UI design, visual hierarchy, spacing, alignment, typography, product photography and consistency. Avoid generic ERP-style dashboards on the public storefront.

### 2. DESIGN DIRECTION

Create a premium Indian government e-commerce website combining:

- Modern marketplace aesthetics.
- Government institutional branding.
- Indian cultural identity.
- Premium product presentation.
- Minimal and clean interface.
- Professional shopping experience.

The design should look like a professionally designed production-ready e-commerce platform, not a generic AI-generated website.

Avoid:
- Excessive gradients.
- Too many colorful cards.
- Overcrowded layouts.
- Unnecessary shadows.
- Oversized rounded components.
- Generic dashboard designs.
- Excessive icons.
- Poor-quality stock photographs.
- Inconsistent spacing.

Use high-quality product photography and visually rich promotional banners.

Create reusable color styles, typography styles, spacing tokens, shadows, border styles and components.
